Will it be cheaper next week?

Summary

The same phone, two prices

Shoppers in Malaysia often see the same phone at very different prices on Lazada and Shopee, and prices swing around sale events like 11.11 and 12.12. PricePulse collects those listings every week, matches them to one product, and answers three questions: where is it cheapest now, how has the price moved, and will it be cheaper next week?

Users can bookmark products and set a target price to get an email when it's reached. Staff have an ML panel to retrain and roll back the model. There's a React Native app too.

What I did

From listings to comparable histories

  • A weekly scraping pipeline (Playwright and BeautifulSoup) on Celery and Redis, writing to MySQL.
  • Cross-platform product matching, so a listing on each marketplace becomes one product with one price history.
  • Features for trend, volatility and regime: stable-zone and anomaly flags, consecutive trend weeks, trend consistency.
  • A Django and Django REST Framework web app, plus the mobile app, alerts and the staff ML panel.

A decision

Pick the metric before the model

About three quarters of product-weeks are stable. A model that never predicts a move scores 76% accuracy and is useless. So I evaluated with expanding-window walk-forward splits (train on every week before t, predict t, move on) and chose on macro F1, which weighs up, down and stable equally.

No model beats the baseline on accuracy. PricePulse v2.0 gives up a few points of it to make a real attempt at catching up and down moves.

Class-level performance

The final model is a Random Forest with class-balanced bootstrap samples, shallow trees and a minimum leaf size, to resist overfitting a small dataset. SMOTE oversampling was tried and dropped. A confidence threshold turns low-confidence up or down calls into stable; it is tuned each run under a floor of 0.50 macro precision, with all three classes still predicted.

Even so, the confusion matrix shows that most real up and down weeks still get predicted as stable. That is the model's main weakness, and the next thing I would work on.
