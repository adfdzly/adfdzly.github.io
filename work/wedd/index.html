Summary

A marketplace where a booking is a promise made months ahead

Wedd is a wedding marketplace and planner for the Malaysian market. Couples find and book vendors. Vendors run their business on it: bookings, availability, team members, packages, reviews, business verification, subscriptions, and a public Wedd Site they publish themselves. Staff moderate the marketplace from an admin console.

A couple often pays a deposit long before the day, the vendor delivers on one day, and the balance lands somewhere in between. The product has to make that stretch of time feel safe for both sides.

What I did

What I built

  • A NestJS 11 API with Prisma on PostgreSQL (Supabase for the database and auth), and Redis for rate limits and cross-instance socket.io broadcast.
  • A React vendor portal that also serves every published vendor Wedd Site, branching on the host name, plus a React admin console for staff.
  • Two Expo / React Native apps: one for couples, one for vendors. A shared auth package keeps sessions consistent across the vendor portal, admin console and vendor app.
  • A Turborepo monorepo with PR-only branching, CI that merges on green, secrets managed per environment, and a self-hosted staging server.

A decision

Booking state and money state are separate machines

The obvious design is one status column on a booking: pending, paid, done. It breaks the first time a couple pays a deposit, then an interim payment, then disputes part of the balance. A single status can't say "confirmed, partly paid, payout held".

So Wedd keeps three tracks that move independently and are linked by events:

  • The booking moves from pending to confirmed, in progress and completed, or ends cancelled or refunded.
  • Each charge (deposit, interim, full balance) has its own payment state, including partial refunds and disputes.
  • The vendor's payout is held after payment and released only after the event and the couple's sign-off, with a review step for disputes.

At each milestone, such as the vendor accepting or a payment arriving, the system stores an evidence snapshot. A refund or dispute never has to rewrite booking history to explain itself.

Any screen in Wedd can tell you who is holding the money right now, and why.
